SFX PREVIEWER

Open MDC and select [S] SFX Previewer.
Browse the left-hand list with Up/Down or search by SFX ID/name.
Use Play/Replay, Pause, Stop, Previous and Next; Space replays the selected effect.
Choose an enemy target and battle arena in the right-hand panel. Both can be hidden.
Sound playback is optional. Preview hit reaction tests the target animation.
Damage numbers are illustrative, not real battle damage calculations.

Escape or F12 returns to MDC. Escape from MDC Home returns to the game.
Previews use dummy actors and do not execute a full battle attack sequence.
Custom multi-effect sequences still need testing in battle.
When opening from battle, enter between attacks. If playback is unavailable,
finish the active attack before opening the preview again.
