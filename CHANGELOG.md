# Changelog

## 1.4.1 · November 23, 2026

- Fixed black backgrounds in the Rendered Field Viewer. It detects the atlas loaded for each field and uses 32 px tiles for original backgrounds or 64 px tiles for Moguri overrides. It also skips the invalid placeholder field when opening.
- Pauses title-screen and gameplay music while MDC is open. Music resumes on close, and audio previews can still play.
- Adds translucent main console buttons inspired by the SFX Previewer.

## 1.4 · November 22, 2026

- Added the Rendered Field Viewer with animated backgrounds, weather, SPS, audio controls, and All Scenarios mode.
- Expanded the SFX Previewer and redesigned the Sound Browser.
- Refreshed the console layout, added saved colors and viewer shortcuts, and improved Live Log filtering.
- Added model-ID FBX export and refined the Field Background Toolkit and World Entrance Creator.
- Updated compatibility with Memoria stable and improved viewer exit, audio cleanup, and navigation.

See the [packaged changelog](Memoria%20Dev%20Console/CHANGELOG.txt) for the full 1.4 notes and earlier releases.
